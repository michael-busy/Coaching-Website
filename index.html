Personal Coaching

Get your thoughts and actions structured

Every day we are trying to achieve more, leading to more thoughts and distractions and the best path gets blurred. Get your thoughts in order for a clear action forward.

How the process works

  1. 1

    Clarify your goals

    Articulate exactly what you want to achieve, when you want to get there and why it matters to you.

  2. 2

    Assess current reality

    Honestly evaluate where you stand today and what's getting in the way.

  3. 3

    Explore your options

    Discover strategies and pathways to move you forward with intent.

  4. 4

    Commit to action

    Establish concrete next steps and the accountability to see them through.

Confidence Building

Confidence is a skill — learn to build it

Trouble saying no, over-analysing, not speaking up in meetings or getting out of control. A mix of personal coaching with a focus on confidence building by improving your control, appearance, posture, attitude and leadership.

The 5 key elements we focus on

  1. 1

    Current confidence level

    Assessment of your confidence level.

  2. 2

    Target setting

    Set the confidence level you want to be at.

  3. 3

    Confidence principles

    Learn about the key confidence principles that build your confidence.

  4. 4

    Practice

    Work on your confidence in real life via exercises.

  5. 5

    Reflect & improve

    Reflect in a session on what went well and what can improve, to set up the next exercises.
